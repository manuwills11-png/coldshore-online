# ColdShore Online: "Salt & Signal"

Co-op for 1 to 6 players. You run a crew out of a shared settlement, take missions into the county, bring back supplies, upgrade the settlement and survive the raids that come for it. The story is separate from the single-player campaign: a different crew, a different threat, and the same county.

## The story

Three weeks after the quarantine, a voice comes over every radio in the county. It calls itself WARDEN. It promises airdrops of food, medicine and ammunition to any group that does its jobs: recover a black case from a clinic, hold a relay mast for ten minutes, burn a nest under a church. Your crew sets up in the empty warehouses of Harbor Row, which you name **Haven**, and takes the deal.

The jobs get stranger. The cases are cold to the touch. Every nest you burn makes the next horde bigger. By the third chapter the crew realises that WARDEN is not feeding survivors. It is running an experiment on them, using the dead to test which groups hold together. The last chapter is a siege on Haven itself, and a choice: keep taking WARDEN's drops, or find the transmitter in New Halden and pull the plug.

| Chapter | Name | Unlocks at | Story operations |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Haven | Start | **First Drop**: recover WARDEN's first airdrop. **Lights On**: restore the generator at Haven. **Harbor Sweep**: clear Harbor Row. |
| 2 | The Offer | Radio 1 | **Cold Case**: recover a black case from the Millbrook clinic. **Mast**: hold the Pine Ridge relay mast. **Burn It**: destroy a nest under the Greywater church. |
| 3 | Pattern | Radio 2, 6 missions done | **Ledger**: find WARDEN's ledger in Fairhaven. **Taken**: rescue the crew of another settlement. **Night Shift**: hold Calder through a whole night. |
| 4 | The Tower | Radio 3, 12 missions done | **Signal Trace**: triangulate from three masts. **Halden Tower**: clear floors in New Halden to reach the roof. |
| 5 | Warden | Radio 4, Chapter 4 done | **Last Drop**: siege on Haven, then the transmitter. The ending is a crew vote: keep the drops or cut the signal. |

Between story operations, the mission board always offers **contracts**: procedurally generated missions in any unlocked town. Contracts pay supplies and keep the threat curve moving.

## Mission types (objectives)

Every mission ends the same way: **get everyone still alive back to the extraction point** (Haven, or a pickup marked on the map). Downed players can be revived by a teammate. A mission fails if the whole crew is down at once, or if a defence point falls.

| Type | Objective | What makes it hard |
|---|---|---|
| **Scavenge** | Find and collect N supply caches hidden in the houses of a town (N grows with crew size). | Caches are spread out, so the crew splits or takes longer. The town is infested. |
| **Airdrop** | A crate falls somewhere in a town. Reach it first, crack it open (hold E, 10 s), then extract. | A flare on the crate draws every zombie in the area. Rivals' runners arrive at higher threat. |
| **Defend** | Hold a point (relay mast, generator, barricade) for 3 to 6 minutes. At least one player must stay inside the circle. | Waves grow every minute, with specials (screamers, bloaters, brutes). |
| **Clear** | Wipe out an infested zone, including its nest. | The nest keeps spawning until destroyed. Brutes guard it. |
| **Convoy** | Drive to a town, pick up the load, drive it back. | Road hordes, pile-ups and grab attempts on every leg. A low-health truck slows the run. |
| **Rescue** | Crack a trapped survivor out (hold E at the door), then hold the spot until they're on their feet. | Every second at the door makes noise. |
| **Hunt** | Track down and kill an apex brute: a huge elite with more than double health. | It hits hard and calls the pack. |
| **Gauntlet** | Run a timed route through several towns. Miss a checkpoint's clock and the run fails. | No time to loot. Roads are blocked. |
| **Blackout** | At night: collect the fuses, then hold the substation while the lights come back. | Dark, fast runners, flashlights only. |
| **Demolition** | Collect the charges, plant them, then get out before they go. | Blast radius and the horde it pulls. |
| **Siege** | WARDEN's experiment comes to Haven. Defend the settlement through waves. | Damage to Haven lowers building levels if you lose. Walls and watchtowers decide how much help you get. |

Clear missions end with **nests**: flesh mounds spread around the town. Hold E at each one to burn it while waves keep coming.

**Bonus objective:** every operation rolls one bonus: nobody goes down, finish inside 9 minutes, kill an elite, or 60 kills between the squad. Meeting it pays 50% more supplies and XP.

## Free choice: who goes where

There are no rules about who goes where. Every player picks for themselves.

- **Deploy** on any contract takes only you. Anyone else can **join** your squad whenever they like, from the board or from the map, or start a different contract of their own.
- Every open contract and every squad already out is marked on the map (M), at its town. Click one to see it, then press Enter to deploy, J to join or W to set a waypoint.
- Clicking anywhere else on the map sets a waypoint: a diamond on screen with the distance, a beam in the world and a dot on the minimap. Right-click or X clears it.
- Each squad has its own sim host, its own zombies and its own objectives. Players at Haven see each other walking around.
- Pulling out of an operation with others still in it leaves the squad running.

## Playing together

- **Humvee: driver and gunner.** Online, one player drives the Humvee and another mans the roof gun. Walk up to a Humvee a teammate is driving and press **E** to climb onto the turret (Shift+E takes the gun on a parked one). The gunner aims with the mouse and holds fire. The gun overheats if held too long, and the driver can't fire while someone is on it. Offline, you drive and shoot yourself (hold fire or F).
- **Riding along.** Press **E** next to any car a teammate is driving to ride in it. Passengers can shoot their own weapon out of the window. E gets you out.
- **Pings.** Press **P** (or click the middle mouse button) to mark where you're looking. Your squad sees an orange beam, your name and the distance for 14 seconds.
- **Sharing.** Press **.** (full stop) next to a teammate to hand over a bandage and half a magazine of your current gun's ammo.
- Players already **revive** each other (hold E on a downed teammate).

## The settlement: Haven

Haven is a real place on the map: a walled yard on the open ground east of the bay. Every operation starts at the gate and ends back inside it. Between operations you can walk around it with the rest of the crew.

Everything you see comes from the building levels saved on the server, so it changes as the crew builds:

- **Walls:** a scrap barricade, then a timber palisade, a walkway, concrete blocks, steel plate and razor wire.
- **Watchtowers:** up to five, with searchlights from level 3.
- **Housing:** tents, then cabins (2 more per level). Settlers salvage scrap while you are offline.
- **Farm:** crop fields and scarecrows, then a chicken coop, a pig pen, a cow pasture, a greenhouse and a windmill. The animals wander, peck and graze.
- **Power:** a generator with an exhaust plume, solar arrays, a wind turbine from level 3, floodlights and string lights over the plaza.
- **Workshop, Infirmary, Garage and Radio mast:** each grows a size with its level.

**Upgrading is done in person at the command table** in the plaza. It lifts the camera over Haven and flies it to whichever building you pick. Each building has a card showing its level pips, what it does now and after the upgrade, the cost (red where you are short), and any lock. Hold Space, or the button, to build. A scaffold goes up, dust rises, the new level grows out of the ground and a banner announces it to the whole crew.

Supplies: **scrap, food, fuel, meds, ammo**. Missions pay them out. The farm makes food over time, housing makes scrap, and power burns fuel.

| Building | What each level gives (levels 1 to 5) | Requires |
|---|---|---|
| **Walls** | 10% smaller siege waves per level. | — |
| **Watchtower** | Gunners on the walls fire at anything near Haven during operations. | Level 3 needs Power 2 |
| **Housing** | +6 settlers and +4 scrap an hour per level. | — |
| **Farm** | +6 food an hour per level. Animals from level 1. | Level 2 needs Housing 1, level 4 needs Housing 3 |
| **Power** | Lights, solar, wind. Needed by other buildings. | — |
| **Workshop** | Weapon damage +6% per level. Issues a shotgun (1), rifle (2), hunting rifle (3) and SMG (5) to everyone who deploys. | Level 2 needs Power 1, level 4 needs Power 3 |
| **Infirmary** | Max health +10 per level, plus free bandages. | Level 3 needs Power 2 |
| **Garage** | Tougher vehicles for convoys. | — |
| **Radio** | Unlocks story chapters and towns farther away. | Level 2 needs Power 1, level 4 needs Power 3 |

## Weapons and the armory

Guns are scarce. You start with a pistol. The workshop issues the standard guns, and everything else has to be found.

Thirty-six weapons, each with a rarity: common, uncommon, rare, epic or legendary.

|---|---|---|
| Nail bat | Common | Staggers anything |
| .357 revolver | Uncommon | Six heavy rounds |
| Sawn-off 12ga | Uncommon | Two barrels, devastating up close |
| Fire axe | Uncommon | Knocks zombies flat |
| Crossbow | Rare | Silent; a bolt goes through one zombie into the next |
| Katana | Rare | Fast, hits three at once |
| Sledgehammer | Rare | Slow; flattens a crowd |
| AK 7.62 | Epic | Hard-hitting full auto |
| M249 SAW | Legendary | 100-round belt |

| Skorpion, MP5, MX4, MP7 | Uncommon to rare | Submachine guns; the MP7 goes through two |
| HK53, AR-15, QBZ-97B, SCAR-H, G36C, M14 | Uncommon to epic | Rifles |

Weapons drop from crates (about 3.5% at the start), specials, brutes and elites (far more often), and airdrops. Both the drop chance and the odds of a rare one climb with campaign level, or with the threat of an operation. A dropped weapon stands in a beam of its rarity colour.

Online, any weapon you pick up goes into your **armory** on the server, and you carry it on every operation after that.

## Difficulty

Each mission has a **threat** level that sets zombie numbers, how many are runners, how often specials appear, and how hard they hit.

`threat = 1 + 0.25 × missions completed + 0.4 × (players − 1) + tier bonus`

Tier bonus is 0 for Normal, +1.5 for Hard and +3 for Nightmare. Harder tiers pay out more.

On top of threat:

- **Elites** (armoured, red-eyed, 4.5× health) join infestations, clears and the last quarter of every hold.
- The last quarter of a hold brings 40% bigger waves and extra brutes.
- From threat 2, runners come out of the trees behind the squad on the way home.

- Threat 1 to 3: mostly shamblers.
- Threat 3 to 6: runners, screamers and night missions appear.
- Threat 6 and up: bloaters, brutes, bigger waves and road events on every drive.

Failing a mission costs a share of the supplies carried, never the settlement.

## Rewards

Each player earns XP and kills for their own profile. The crew earns supplies, chapter progress and new board missions. Rewards scale with threat and tier.
